About the project

Building a clearer view of traffic controls

Road Safety Zambia

This system is being developed to support large-scale analysis of road markings, road signs, and road video footage in Zambia.

Current stage

The system now supports authorized uploads, public access to uploaded road videos, stored frame analysis, and results summaries for traffic-sign and road-marking findings when a compatible model is available.

Model support is explicit: we only display classes supported by the configured inference model, not generic, unsupported classes.
